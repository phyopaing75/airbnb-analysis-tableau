## Project Overview

* Live Dashboard: [View on Tableau Public](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/phyo.paing8212/viz/airbnb_analysis_17901513018730/Dashboard1)
* Dataset: [Access Dataset on Google Drive](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1pmh1SwwtRqr5Ad_hs3j6jdF9WpR8dMIK)
* Tools & Technologies: Tableau Desktop, Tableau Public, Microsoft Excel
* Dataset Scope: Comprehensive Seattle Airbnb market dataset (`airbnb_data.xlsx`) comprising 3,818 unique property listings, 84,849 customer reviews, and over 1,000,000 calendar availability and booking records across 2016.
* Business Objective: Short-term rental hosts and real estate investors often struggle with dynamic rate setting, seasonal variance, and hyper-local location premiums. This project consolidates listing metrics, calendar booking activity, and geographic parameters into an interactive Tableau dashboard, empowering hosts to identify optimal price points by property size, track seasonal revenue peaks, and evaluate neighborhood supply dynamics.

## Key Metrics & Business Insights

* Geographic Pricing Premiums: Distinct geographic zones command major rate differences across Seattle postal codes. Core downtown, coastal, and tourist-adjacent zip codes reach average nightly prices upwards of $160–$200, whereas outer suburban areas normalize around $70–$90.
* Property Size Elasticity: Average price scales predictably with bedroom capacity—moving from $96.2/night for 1-bedroom properties up to $584.8/night for 6-bedroom units. Large multi-bedroom assets command exponentially higher nightly yields.
* Supply Distribution: Market inventory is heavily concentrated in smaller formats, with 1-bedroom (1,811 units) and 2-bedroom (483 units) listings comprising the bulk of the market. Accommodations with 5 or more bedrooms represent fewer than 75 listings citywide, highlighting a low-supply opportunity for group travel.
* Annual Revenue Trends: Weekly calendar revenue ramps up aggressively starting in late winter (February–March), reaches peak sustained booking yields through mid-summer (June–August), and remains elevated through late autumn.
